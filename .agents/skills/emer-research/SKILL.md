## 1. High-Margin Product Research (AU Market Focus)

When researching and validating products for the Australian market:

### Target Metrics
- **Minimum Gross Margin**: 35% - 50% (after eBay FVF, PayPal/Managed Payments fee of ~13-15%, ad spend, and GST).
- **Sell-Through Rate (STR)**: > 50% within 30 days for target keywords on eBay AU.
- **Competition Density**: Avoid items where > 20 sellers are competing at rock-bottom prices with identical stock images unless there is an angle for bundling, faster AU domestic shipping, or superior SEO.
- **Seasonality & Geography**: Account for Australian seasons (inverted vs. Northern Hemisphere), AU holidays (Click Frenzy, Boxing Day, EOFY - End of Financial Year in June, Black Friday, Father's Day in Sept, Mother's Day in May).

### Product Selection Criteria Checklist
- [ ] Lightweight or easy-to-ship (reduces Australia Post parcel rates).
- [ ] Low return rate category (avoid high-defect electronics or complex-fit apparel unless sizing charts are bulletproof).
- [ ] Solves a distinct problem or fits into high-demand Australian lifestyle niches (outdoor/camping, 4WD, DIY/home improvement, pet care, gardening, fitness).
- [ ] Has reliable Australian domestic warehouse availability (Sydney/Melbourne 2-4 day dispatch preferred).

## 2. Cassini Algorithm & Listing Optimization (SEO)

eBay’s Cassini search algorithm prioritizes **relevance, conversion rate (sales velocity), customer service metrics, and complete item specifics**.

### Title Formula (Strict 80-Character Maximum)
```
[Main Keyword] [Secondary High-Search Keyword] [Key Attribute/Size/Colour] [Primary Benefit/AU Stock] [Model/Compatibility]
```
- *Example*: `Heavy Duty Waterproof Pet Car Seat Cover Dog Rear Protector Mat Hammock AU Stock`
- **Rules**:
  - No punctuation or keyword stuffing with meaningless symbols (`***`, `LOOK!`, `L@@K`).
  - Maximize the full 80 characters without truncation.
  - Front-load high-search-volume terms in the first 40 characters for mobile display.

### Item Specifics & Attributes
- Fill out **100% of required and recommended item specifics** (Brand, MPN, Material, Dimensions, Type, Color, Features).
- Custom item specifics increase visibility in eBay filter facets.

### High-Converting Listing Structure
1. **Clear Trust Badges**: "Australian Owned & Operated", "Fast Domestic Dispatch from NSW/VIC", "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee".
2. **Feature-to-Benefit Bullets**: Explain why the product improves the buyer's life.
3. **Specifications Table**: Clean HTML / responsive layout without active content (no JavaScript, no external links).
4. **Shipping & Return Policy Clear Breakdown**: Specify Australia Post / Express options and warranty details.

## 3. Promoted Listings & Ad Campaign Strategy

### Campaign Tiers

1. **Promoted Listings Standard (PLS) - Dynamic / General Coverage**:
   - Set ad rate at **2% - 5%** (above baseline to trigger visibility boost without wiping margin).
   - Use for wide catalog items to drive organic Cassini rank via initial sales velocity.

2. **Promoted Listings Advanced (PLA) - Target CPC for Top 10% Winners**:
   - Focus on high-intent exact and phrase match keywords.
   - Target top-of-search placements for validated bestseller items.
   - Continuous negative keyword pruning weekly.

3. **Promotions Manager & Volume Discounts**:
   - **Multi-Buy**: "Buy 2 Save 5%, Buy 3 Save 10%" (increases Average Order Value).
   - **Order Discount**: "Spend $50 Save $5".
   - **Markdown Events**: Weekend flash sales / EOFY promotional banners.

## 4. Bulk Importing, Inventory Sync & Supplier Relations

### Supplier Sourcing Guidelines
- **Domestic Sourcing**: Partner with verified AU local suppliers / warehouses (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane) for 1-3 day handling and 2-5 day delivery.
- **Overseas / Direct Dropshipping**: Utilize reliable suppliers with specialized AU shipping lines (e.g., UBI Smart Parcel, Yanwen AU Express, Australia Post eParcel direct lines).
- **Negotiation Strategy**:
  - Negotiate volume discounts at 50+ monthly orders.
  - Request custom packaging / unbranded inserts.
  - Agree on Service Level Agreements (SLAs): 24-48h tracking upload window to maintain Top Rated Seller (TRS) status on eBay AU.

### Bulk Import & CSV Architecture
- Standardize data feeds (Title, SKU, Barcode/EAN, Price, Stock Buffer, Description, Image URLs).
- Always maintain an **Inventory Safety Buffer** (e.g., minimum stock display = actual supplier stock - 5) to prevent out-of-stock defects.
